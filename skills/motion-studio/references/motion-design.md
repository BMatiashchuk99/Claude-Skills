# Motion design cheat sheet

**Structure (30 s short):** hook (0-3 s) → problem or promise → 2-4 proof/feature scenes → payoff → CTA.
For 16:9 explainers allow 60-90 s and 6-12 scenes.

**Hook ideas:** a surprising number (`stat`), a bold 3-word claim (`kinetic` with `slam`),
a before/after (`compare`), a question the viewer silently answers.

**Text:** max ~6 words on screen per scene; the voice carries the detail. One highlighted
word per headline. Sentence case reads friendlier, uppercase reads louder.

**Pacing:** cut every 2-4 s on vertical, 3-6 s on horizontal. Fast scenes → `whip`/`push`/`slide`;
calm moments → `fade`/`blur`; reveals → `iris`/`zoom`; hard emphasis → `cut` (adds an impact hit).
Don't repeat one transition more than twice in a row.

**Camera:** `drift` keeps static text alive, `push-in` builds focus, `orbit`/`tilt` suit
screenshots and cards, `shake` only for impact moments.

**Color:** one accent for emphasis, a second accent for gradients only. Keep body text `fg`
on `bg`; never accent-on-accent. On `paper` (light) use darker accents.

**Vertical safe area:** platform UI covers the bottom ~20% and right edge; captions sit
above it, scenes keep their content in the upper two thirds automatically.

**Sound:** whooshes on transitions, pops on list items, a riser into a big number,
a click on the CTA - all automatic. Keep music around -16 dB under voice; it is ducked anyway.

**Checklist before final render:** stills reviewed · no text cut off · every scene has a
clear focal point · CTA readable for 2+ s · voice provider is a natural one.
